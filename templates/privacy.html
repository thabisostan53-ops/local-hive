Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Local Hive, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using Local Hive, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📋 What Information We Collect

  • Listing Information: Title, description, price, category, and location you provide when creating a listing
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, or WhatsApp number you choose to share
  • Images: Photos you upload for your listings
  • Technical Data: Basic browser information for website functionality

🔒 How We Use Your Information

  • To display your listings on the website
  • To facilitate communication between buyers and sellers
  • To maintain website security and prevent spam
  • To improve our services

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

🛡️ Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We recommend:

  • Meeting in public places for transactions
  • Never sharing sensitive financial information through listings
  • Being cautious about what personal information you include

⏰ Data Retention

Listings automatically expire after 30 days. After expiration, listing data may be retained for:

  • Fraud prevention purposes (up to 12 months)
  • Legal compliance requirements
  • Website improvement analytics

🍪 Cookies

Local Hive uses minimal cookies for:

  • Session management (remembering admin login)
  • Basic analytics to improve user experience

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request removal of your listing at any time
  • Contact us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of any future communications

To exercise these rights, use the report function on any listing or contact the administrator.

📞 Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through:

  • Admin dashboard: /admin
  • Report function on any listing

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Significant changes will be announced on the homepage.
