About Local Hive

Building stronger communities through local commerce

🐝 Our Mission

Local Hive is a community marketplace where neighbors can buy and sell items without the need for accounts or complicated sign-ups. We believe in building stronger communities through local commerce.

💡 Why Local Hive?

By connecting neighbors, we reduce waste, save money, and build relationships. Whether you're selling a vintage bicycle or looking for a new bookshelf, Local Hive helps you connect with people right in your neighborhood.

🌍 Our Values

  • 🤝 Community First: We prioritize local connections over global reach
  • 🔒 Privacy by Design: No accounts required, you control what you share
  • ♻️ Sustainability: Keep items in use and reduce waste
  • 💸 No Fees: Free to list and free to buy
